Spare Change for Spirits
Opening music and narration

The story is written for this game. Its translations are in assets/i18n.
The four-bar score is composed and synthesised in tools/make_sounds.py.
The evening tunes and night ambiences of every season are composed and
synthesised in the same script.
The distributed voice files are finished readings of the three lines.
Synthesis took place during production. The game plays local Ogg audio.

Speech sources

German uses Thorsten Müller’s German voice recordings, released under CC0.
https://github.com/thorstenMueller/Thorsten-Voice

English uses the public-domain LJ Speech Dataset, recorded by Linda Johnson
and prepared by Keith Ito.
https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/

Czech, Dutch, Polish, Brazilian and European Portuguese, Romanian, Spanish
from Spain, Turkish and both Mandarin readings use voices from the Open Home
Foundation voice collection. The source recordings are released under CC0.
https://github.com/OHF-Voice/voice-datasets

The Latin American Spanish reading uses the Mexican Spanish recordings
published by rmcpantoja under the Unlicense.
https://huggingface.co/datasets/rmcpantoja/Ald_Mexican_Spanish_speech_dataset
https://unlicense.org/

Danish, Norwegian and Swedish use recordings distributed by the National
Library of Norway under CC0. Finnish uses the Finnish single-speaker
recordings distributed by Bryan Park under CC0.
https://www.nb.no/sprakbanken/en/resource-catalogue/oai-nb-no-sbr-21/
https://www.nb.no/sprakbanken/en/resource-catalogue/oai-nb-no-sbr-15/
https://www.nb.no/sprakbanken/en/resource-catalogue/oai-nb-no-sbr-17/
https://www.kaggle.com/bryanpark/finnish-single-speaker-speech-dataset
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Arabic, Bulgarian, French, Greek, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese,
Korean, Malay, Russian, Ukrainian and Vietnamese use preset voices from
Supertone Inc. Their speech-generation weights are licensed under Open
RAIL-M. The Malay reading uses the language-agnostic mode.
https://huggingface.co/Supertone/supertonic-3/blob/main/LICENSE

The Thai reading uses PyThaiNLP’s offline Thai voice, released under
the Apache License 2.0.
https://huggingface.co/pythainlp/MakHamTTS
https://github.com/PyThaiNLP/PyThaiTTS

The speech rendering tool sherpa-onnx is licensed under Apache 2.0.
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/blob/master/LICENSE
