# Legacy 2D images

Images from the 2D version of the shop: furniture and keepsakes (`decor/`) and the door, counter
and back-wall background (`shop/`). Since the switch to the 3D diorama these are 3D models
(`assets/models/`). The furniture and keepsake pictures live on as button icons: reduced copies
(96 × 96, made with `tools/prepare_sprite.py <file> decor <id>`) are in `assets/sprites/decor/`.
The originals and the door, counter and background images are kept here, outside the packaged
`assets/`, for reference and promotional use.
